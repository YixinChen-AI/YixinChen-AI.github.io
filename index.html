I am a PhD candidate at Peking University. I develop medical imaging methods across PET, CT, and MRI, with work on universal segmentation, cross-modality adaptation, and interactive PET/CT lesion segmentation. I also study metabolic patterns in total-body PET/CT.

My work has appeared in Nature Communications, IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, and MICCAI. I lead an NSFC-funded doctoral project on total-body PET/CT metabolic analysis. I welcome conversations about research collaborations and clinical imaging applications.
