I am a third-year PhD candidate at Peking University, working on medical imaging foundation models across modalities (PET, MRI, CT, ultrasound) and diseases (Alzheimer’s, cancer, cardiac, MASLD). My research moves from cross-modality domain adaptation (FVP, PCNet) toward universal segmentation and multimodal foundation models at scale.

I have published 11 peer-reviewed papers in venues including Nature Communications, IEEE TMI (×2), MICCAI, and IEEE TRPMS. My most recent work, MPUM (Nature Communications, 2025), introduces a modality-projection universal model that segments 215+ anatomical structures across full-body CT, MRI and PET from a single network trained on 33K+ scans. My doctoral research is supported by the NSFC Doctoral Research Project.

Before PKU I worked on clinical AI at BioMind (2020–2022) and AirDoc (2022). I earned an MSc with Distinction in Informatics from King’s College London (2019–2020), and a BSc in Automation from Xiamen University (2015–2019).

MPUM
Modality-Projection
Universal Model
Modality-projection universal model for comprehensive full-body medical imaging segmentation
Yixin Chen, et al.
Nature Communications, 2025
[Paper] [arXiv] [Code]
ProblemMedical image segmentation models are typically modality- or region-specific, unable to generalize across CT, MRI, and PET in one unified framework. MethodA modality-projection mechanism aligns heterogeneous inputs to a shared representation; a single network segments 215+ anatomical structures, pre-trained on 33K+ multi-modal scans. ImpactAn open-source full-body medical imaging foundation model with zero-shot transfer to unseen modalities and tasks, lowering the cost of deploying segmentation AI in hospitals.
FVP
Fourier Visual Prompting
FVP: Fourier visual prompting for source-free unsupervised domain adaptation of medical image segmentation
Yan Wang, Jian Cheng, Yixin Chen, Shuai Shao, Lanyun Zhu, Zhenzhou Wu, Tao Liu, Haogang Zhu
IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, 2023
[DOI]
71 citations
ProblemIn source-free unsupervised domain adaptation (SFUDA) for medical segmentation, the model cannot access source data and target data is unlabeled — the common reality in hospitals. MethodFourier Visual Prompting (FVP): a small learnable prompt added to the Fourier amplitude spectrum of target images, so the frozen source model performs well on the target domain without any retraining. ImpactLightweight, source-data-free adaptation that respects medical data privacy and works as plug-and-play for off-the-shelf segmentation models in cross-center deployment.

PCNet: Prior category network for CT universal segmentation model
Yixin Chen, Yajuan Gao, Lei Zhu, Wenrui Shao, Yanye Lu, Hongbin Han, Zhaoheng Xie
IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, 2024
[DOI] [Code]

ProblemCT segmentation models trained per-task or per-organ struggle to scale to universal multi-organ settings. MethodPrior Category Network (PCNet) injects organ-category priors into the decoder, enabling a single network to predict any organ within a universal label space. ImpactA practical universal CT segmentation backbone that handles dozens of organ classes from one model, reducing the engineering cost of clinical multi-task segmentation pipelines.

LUCIDA: Low-dose universal-tissue CT image domain adaptation for medical segmentation
Yixin Chen, et al.
MICCAI, 2024
8 citations
ProblemLow-dose CT is essential for reducing radiation exposure but noise degrades segmentation accuracy, and models trained on standard-dose CT transfer poorly. MethodLUCIDA: a low-dose universal-tissue CT image domain adaptation framework that aligns low-dose to standard-dose representations across multiple tissues without paired data. ImpactEnables accurate multi-tissue segmentation on low-dose CT, supporting safer clinical screening workflows.
