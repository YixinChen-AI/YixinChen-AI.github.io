# World map source

`assets/img/world.svg` is the unmodified world map from Victor Cazanave's SVG Maps project, based on MapSVG, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

- Source: https://github.com/VictorCazanave/svg-maps/tree/master/packages/world
- Original map: https://mapsvg.com/maps/world
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The homepage creates an interactive copy of its country paths, with visit-count colors and translated geographic names. The source geometry remains unchanged. It loads only when the visitor section approaches the viewport.
